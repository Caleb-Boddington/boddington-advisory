Who

Who looks after your information

Caleb Boddington, trading as Boddington Advisory, a sole trader in England. For anything on this page, email caleb@boddingtonadvisory.co.uk.

This site

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. The fonts and images all come from this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else to show it.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep short technical logs of visits, including IP addresses, to keep its service secure. That is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Emails

If you email me

I get your name, your email address and whatever you choose to write. I use it to reply, and if we agree some work, to do that work and keep the records a business has to keep.

I don't sell it, share it for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

Email to this address is forwarded by Porkbun to a Google Gmail account, and replies are sent through Brevo. Those companies handle it for me and may store it outside the UK, under the safeguards UK data protection law requires.

How long

How long I keep it

An enquiry that doesn't lead to any work is deleted within 12 months. If we work together, the records are kept for as long as HMRC requires, which is currently about six years.

Your rights

What you can ask for

You can ask to see what I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. Email caleb@boddingtonadvisory.co.uk and I'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
